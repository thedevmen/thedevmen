About

Two riders.
One mission.

We spot gaps in the mobile gaming and iGaming market—and build businesses to fill them.

At The Developmen, we turn opportunities into minimum viable products, test them with real users, and refine until we have evidence they work. Once validated, we move fast—scaling aggressively across multiple markets, with a clear exit strategy from the outset.

Ideas are easy.
Execution is everything.

We built, launched, and now run Whossa—a profitable product operating across 16 markets. We know how to bring a product to market, acquire users, and turn growth into a sustainable business.

Behind it all? The two of us, supported by a handful of freelancers. A lean team with hands-on experience across development, growth strategy, and day-to-day operations.

And so it begins.

Lottery Clash

Lottery Clash is lottery reinvented for the mobile generation

  • Pick your tier!

    Three games, three stakes. Start with a $1 scratch, step up to the $5 board game or go for the $10 jackpot draw. Every ticket feeds the Grand Jackpot, which grows every second.

  • Scratch, match, win!

    Scratch Rush is the classic, done right. Scratch all nine tiles and match three to win, with a $1,000 jackpot on every ticket.

  • Ride through Lucky Valley!

    Power Play turns the lottery into a board game. Roll the dice, collect cash and multipliers, dodge the dynamite and race for the Grand Chest.

  • Build your rows!

    In Jackpot Strike you pick seven numbers, or let us pick for you, and join the Grand Jackpot draw. Mini draws with sponsored prizes, like a brand new car, keep things exciting in between.

  • Play together,
    win together!

    Team up with up to ten friends, cover more numbers and share the win. Chat with your crew and climb the team leaderboard together.
